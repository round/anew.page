# Support

Email <maxim@flora.ai> with a question, a bug, or a page that will not encode. Include the URL you
were given, or the HTML you were trying to publish, and what you expected to happen. You can also
[open an issue](https://github.com/round/anew.page/issues).

anew.page is maintained by one person, so replies are not instant, but every message is read. There
is no account to recover and no data to delete — a page lives in its URL, so nothing is held on your
behalf.

To report a security issue, use the same address and put "security" in the subject line. Please do
not publish an exploitable finding before it is fixed.

Reference documentation is in [DOCS.md](DOCS.md); the machine-readable specification is at
[llms.txt](https://anew.page/llms.txt).
