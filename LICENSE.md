## Privacy

- **Stored.** No page content — the URL is the page, and no copy is kept. Because the URL encodes the page, it appears in ordinary request logs like any requested URL.
- **Processed.** Standard request metadata (IP address, user agent, requested URL) is used to serve requests, enforce per-IP rate limits, block flagged content, and count usage in aggregate. No accounts, no tracking cookies, no sale of data.
- **Public previews.** A successful write may render a screenshot of the page, cached publicly at the URL's `.png` twin.
- **Third parties.** The service runs on Cloudflare and is subject to Cloudflare's processing. Outbound links in pages may be checked against Cloudflare's and Google's threat feeds.
- **Retained.** No retention or deletion schedule is promised.
- **No guarantee of privacy or security.** Anyone holding a URL can decode the page in it.
- **Contact.** The [issue tracker](https://github.com/round/anew.page/issues).
