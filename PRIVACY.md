# Privacy & terms

*Last updated 31 August 2026*

anew.page turns a self-contained HTML document into a URL that carries it. No accounts, no
sign-in, no database of pages — the URL is the page. Using the service accepts what follows.

## Privacy

- **Collected.** Nothing you write is stored. Requests carry what any web request carries, and an
  anew URL carries its page; requests are used only to operate the service.
- **Retention.** No timeline and no deletion schedule is promised. There is no stored page to
  delete: stop sharing a URL and it is unreachable.
- **Shared.** With no one. Cloudflare processes requests as the host.
- **Your control.** Privacy mode puts the document in the URL fragment, which never reaches the
  server at all.
- **No guarantee of privacy or security.** That is the nature of the service. A page is its URL:
  anyone holding one holds the page, and URLs travel. Do not put secrets in a page.
- **Contact.** The [issue tracker](https://github.com/round/anew.page/issues). No response is
  promised.

## Terms

  what you encode and for who you share the link with.
- **Acceptable use.** Nothing unlawful; no impersonation, malware, phishing, or credential
  harvesting; no targeting a private individual; no disrupting the service or defeating its
  limits.
- **The service.** Free, unauthenticated, with no uptime and no support commitment. It may change,
  be limited, or be withdrawn at any time; a withdrawal stops future encoding and decoding, not
  any copy of a page you already hold.
- **No warranty, no liability.** Provided "as is" and "as available", with no warranty of any
